Welcome to The Chef's Table

Located in the heart of Colombo 03 at 19 Walukarama Road, The Chef's Table brings together a passion for handcrafted fine dining, vibrant Southeast Asian flavors, and cozy acoustic ambiance.

Whether you are craving our signature Chef’s Special Nasi Goreng, authentic Thai Green Curry, or handcrafted Gelato made in-house by owner Andre, every dish is crafted with fresh, premium ingredients. Bring your favorite bottle along—we are proudly BYOB friendly!
